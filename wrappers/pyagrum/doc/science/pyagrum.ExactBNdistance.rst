ExactBNdistance computes exactly, by enumerating the joint configurations, the Kullback-Leibler divergences
:cite:`kullback1951information` KL(P||Q) and KL(Q||P), the Hellinger distance :cite:`hellinger1909neue` (in the form used by :cite:`kokolakis2001bayesian`), the
Bhattacharyya distance :cite:`kailath1967divergence` and the Jensen-Shannon divergence :cite:`lin1991divergence`
between two Bayesian networks.
