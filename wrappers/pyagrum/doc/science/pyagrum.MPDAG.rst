An MPDAG (maximally oriented PDAG) represents the DAGs of a Markov equivalence class
:cite:`verma1990equivalence` that contain some imposed arcs (background knowledge): it is the closure of the PDAG under
the Meek rules :cite:`meek1995causal`. The MPDAGs and their use with background knowledge are studied in
:cite:`perkovic2017interpreting`; the closure and the consistent extension are computed with the algorithms of
:cite:`wienobst2021extendability`.
