MCBNDistance estimates the Kullback-Leibler divergences :cite:`kullback1951information`, the Hellinger distance
:cite:`hellinger1909neue` (in the form used by :cite:`kokolakis2001bayesian`), the Bhattacharyya distance :cite:`kailath1967divergence` and the Jensen-Shannon divergence
:cite:`lin1991divergence` between two Bayesian networks with independent samples drawn from the first one (forward
sampling in topological order).
