A chain graph is a graph with arcs and undirected edges without partially directed cycle
:cite:`lauritzen1989graphical,frydenberg1990chain,andersson2001alternative,wienobst2021extendability`. Its independence
model depends on its interpretation (LWF, AMP or MVR, :cite:`sonntag2015chain`). The moralization and the c-separation
of this class follow the LWF interpretation :cite:`lauritzen1989graphical,frydenberg1990chain`; the c-separation and
its completeness are from :cite:`bouckaert1995chain,studeny1998chain`.
