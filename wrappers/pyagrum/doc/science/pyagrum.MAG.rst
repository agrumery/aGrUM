A MAG (maximal ancestral graph) represents the independence model of a DAG over its observed variables, the latent
variables being marginalized and the selected ones conditioned on; its separation is the m-separation. The MAGs, their
m-separation and the construction of the MAG of a DAG are from :cite:`richardson2002ancestral`. The causal reasoning
with ancestral graphs is studied in :cite:`zhang2008causal`, and the representative MAG of a PAG is built as in
:cite:`zhang2008completeness` (Theorem 2).
